# Data Sources and Provenance

All datasets in this directory are transcribed from primary sources listed below. Data current as of **November 17, 2026**.

## iie_fall_2026_snapshot.csv

**Source:** Institute of International Education, *Fall 2026 Snapshot on International Student Enrollment: Key Findings* (November 2026).
<https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/IIE_Fall-Snapshot_2026_Key-Findings_2026.pdf>

- Survey of 809 U.S. higher education institutions, conducted by IIE with ten partner higher education associations.
- Covers the 2026/27 academic year; represents a subset of the nearly 3,000 institutions surveyed by Open Doors. The full national picture will be published in Open Doors 2027.
- Year-over-year percentage changes as reported by IIE: total incl. OPT −9%, new enrollments −15%, undergraduate −3%, graduate −15%, OPT −6%.

## reasons_for_decline.csv

**Source:** same IIE Fall 2026 Snapshot. Percentages refer to the share of institutions reporting declines in **new international student enrollments** that identified each factor as contributing. Institutions could cite multiple factors; figures are institutional attributions, not estimated effect shares.

## open_doors_totals.csv

**Sources:**
- 2024/25 (1,177,766, +4.5%): IIE, *Open Doors 2025: Fast Facts* — <https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/OD25_Fast-Facts.pdf>
- 2025/26 (1,146,163, −2.7%): Open Doors 2026 annual release, reported by Higher Ed Dive — <https://www.highereddive.com/news/international-student-enrollment-fell-27-in-2025-26/874661/>
- 2022/23 and 2023/24: IIE Open Doors annual reports (2023, 2024).

## nafsa_economic_impact.csv

**Sources:**
- 2025/26 actuals ($39.9B, 309,409 jobs) and 2024/25 actuals ($42.9B, 355,736 jobs): NAFSA and JB International, press release of November 16, 2026 — <https://www.nafsa.org/about/about-nafsa/international-students-contributed-399-billion-us-economy-2025-2026-fall-2026>
- 2023/24 actuals ($43.8B, 378,175 jobs) and the 2026/27 projection ($37.5B, 278,208 jobs; 1,053,668 students): NAFSA, *Fall 2026 International Student Enrollment Snapshot & Economic Impact Overview* — <https://www.nafsa.org/sites/default/files/media/document/Fall%202026%20International%20Student%20Enrollment%20Snapshot%20Economic%20Impact%20Overview%20FINAL_0.pdf>
- Methodology (one job per ~3 international students; sectors covered): NAFSA, *The Economic Value of International Student Enrollment: Methodology* — <https://www.nafsa.org/sites/default/files/media/document/NAFSA_Methodology_Economic_Value_2025_FINAL%5B1%5D.pdf>
- NAFSA's dollars/jobs calculations exclude students on OPT.

## Caveats

- The IIE Snapshot is a preliminary institutional survey, not a final national count; no confidence intervals or sampling weights are published in the key-findings document.
- NAFSA 2026/27 figures are projections based on the Snapshot, not actuals.
- Stakeholder-authored sources (university associations, advocacy organizations) are used for litigation facts; court outcomes cited are matters of public record, but harm characterizations by party organizations are self-reports.
